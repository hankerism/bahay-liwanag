Why stay with us

A Retreat Designed for Rest

Escape the noise and embrace a slower pace of life. Bahay Liwanag brings together thoughtfully crafted villas, stone pathways, and lantern-lit courtyards set against stunning mountain scenery.

Every detail is curated to help you disconnect from the city and reconnect with what matters most — quiet mornings, warm light, and the people you love.

Our story

Where Heritage Meets the Hills

Nestled among rolling hills and breathtaking mountain views, Bahay Liwanag is a peaceful escape inspired by Filipino heritage architecture and the art of slow living.

Each villa blends natural materials, capiz-inspired windows, and warm interiors with panoramic scenery — a space to watch the sunrise from your deck, gather around a fire pit beneath the stars, or savor a quiet morning with coffee and mountain air.
